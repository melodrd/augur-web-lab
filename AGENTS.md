## Motion and access

Transform/opacity and SVG stroke draws only. For interaction-driven layout changes such as widening picker cards, use FLIP or same-document View Transitions with an instant fallback. Looping scenes are one choreography shared with Figma: keys on a 12 s cycle that open and close on the static artwork (`choreography.ts`; tenets in `tenet-choreography.ts`, Figma `250:717`; hero in `hero-choreography.ts`, Figma `382:848`; REP rows in `rep-choreography.ts`). The three tenet diagrams are always moving while any part is visible (owner, 5–6 Oct 2026: alive, no start-stop-wait, `loop-scene.ts`); SVG animates `transform`, never the individual `translate`/`scale` properties, which re-layerize every frame in Chromium, and avoids per-frame paint such as `stroke-dashoffset`. After arrival the hero tells its 12 s story on compositor-only HTML squares, answers fine pointers with a lens and clicks or taps with a ripple (`hero-ambient.ts`). The roadmap Now stop keeps a slow CSS pulse. Other authored scenes play once per tab session and settle on interaction, reduced motion, print or navigation; a reload (F5) or history visit never replays any of them, read from the navigation type so it holds where storage is blocked, and a return visit never replays a scene already seen (`seen.ts`). Two exceptions play on every load, a reload included (owner, 6 Oct 2026): the hero's “decentralized” underline, which sweeps in and out and answers hover, and the answer-band ribbon, which sweeps once when it scrolls into view. On the review copies, How it works plays a step's scene every time that step is picked, a reload included, because a pick is the reader asking for it; only its entrance waits for a fresh visit (owner, 26 Oct 2026). Its steps are picked by press or key, never hover. What can Augur Lituus power's cards loop instead (owner, 28 Oct 2026: “all animations should be going in a loop”): one choreography on the 12 s cycle that opens and closes on the finished card, with one part lit at a time (`useCaseTimeline.loop`). It runs while the stage is on screen, a reload included; a pick starts the picked case from the top, pressing the case already shown restarts it, and a press, focus or key in the card stops it on the finished card until the next pick. No entrance animation plays on a reload; loops simply run from their resting frame. Hero arrival settles when the page is hidden; ongoing and timed scenes pause when hidden or off-screen and resume only while visible. No-JavaScript, reduced motion and print render final states. Measure any new ongoing motion. Never hide content behind animation. One exception to the motion rules (owner, 22 Oct 2026): from 1024 px, the review copies' hero draws a live canvas floor (`hero-floor-v2.js`), still first, answering fine pointers with a light and clicks or taps with a report; the generated video it replaced stays one switch away in `Hero.astro`. Both are outside the Figma choreography.

Use one tabs controller with keyboard arrows, Home/End, activation mode and labelled panels. Native disclosure buttons and exclusive named details must work by keyboard; no ARIA menu navigation. Native mobile dialog must manage focus. Forms have labels, autocomplete, associated errors and a polite summary. Email composition is not delivery. The newsletter keeps its designed field as a local-only prototype until a provider and consent are approved: it validates in the browser, never sends data and never claims a subscription.
