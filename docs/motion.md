## Shared policy

`play-once.ts` starts a scene when it enters the viewport and cleans up after completion/pagehide. Interaction settles the region; reduced motion cancels it to the static end state. Content and art are fully visible before enhancement and remain settled with JavaScript disabled or in print. Only transform, opacity and SVG stroke draws animate. The picker may change layout only as an intentional interaction with an instant fallback. No scroll hijacking or information gated by animation. Scrolling is plain native scrolling on every page; the homepage section settle was removed on 19 Oct 2026 at the owner's request.

Entrance scenes use the shared play-once controller and play once per tab session. A reload (F5) or a back/forward visit shows every entrance scene settled, read from the navigation type (`PerformanceNavigationTiming.type`), so it holds where `sessionStorage` is blocked: the 6 October report came from a browser that denies storage, where the earlier storage-only rule replayed everything on F5. On a fresh visit `seen.ts` records each scene (by page and script order) in `sessionStorage` when it starts, so a later visit in the same session shows it settled while scenes not yet reached still play; without storage a fresh visit plays them. The hero arrival records `augur:hero-arrived` and also skips on any non-`navigate` navigation type. Two exceptions play on every load, a reload included, at the owner's request (6 Oct 2026): the hero's “decentralized” underline sweep and the answer-band ribbon, which sweeps when it scrolls into view. Looping scenes use `loop-scene.ts`: a scene runs whenever any part of it is on screen, pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab and resumes where it was; every track starts and ends on the static artwork, runs linearly with easing on the keyframes, and may be offset by a positive delay (before its first iteration it shows the static artwork). Development-only `?motion=slow` multiplies timings by five; `?motion=replay` assists comparison. Production ignores those flags.

The markup is the settled page. The script arms a group (`data-armed`, opacity 0 in `home-v2-only.css`) only if it lies wholly below the fold when the script runs, and lifts the attribute as the group lands (Web Animations, `fill: backwards`, cancelled when finished, so settling is `finish()`). Nothing is armed without JavaScript, under reduced motion or forced colours, after a reload or a history visit (navigation type), on an arrival at a `#section`, or for a group already seen this tab session (`seen.ts`'s rules and storage key, mirrored inline). Print shows every group whatever the script is doing. A jump (End, an anchor, find in page) never leaves a blank: every armed group above the last one due lands first, a group already above the screen is simply there, and groups of different sections entering together play without the stagger. Keyboard focus moving into an armed group, a hash change, `beforeprint`, `pagehide` and a reduced-motion or forced-colours change settle every group at once. Each group fires `revealed` as it lands, and the scenes wait for it: the Why Augur scenes and the use-case card start looping once their card or stage has landed, and the answer ribbon, How it works' “paid” and the roadmap track start once the section's lead has landed (How it works' scene and the roadmap track only once their stage or stops have stayed on screen for 120 ms, so an End key or a jump never spends them off screen). A reveal and a loop never start together.
